About Piroma

Built for communities that want better moderation and smoother support.

Piroma was created to make server management easier, cleaner, and more automated without sacrificing quality or control.

Our mission

We believe Discord communities deserve tools that are powerful enough for serious moderation, simple enough for everyday use, and reliable enough to scale with growing communities.

Whether you run a gaming community, support network, or public server, Piroma gives your staff the structure they need to respond faster, manage rules consistently, and automate the repetitive tasks that distract from real engagement.

What Piroma helps with

  • Moderation tools that keep activity organized and consistent
  • Ticket systems that make support feel professional
  • Action logging for transparency and accountability
  • Server automation to reduce repetitive admin work
